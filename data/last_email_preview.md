## 1. Beyond the noise: Early identification of emerging technologies through weak signal prediction in higher-order networks

- 标题: Beyond the noise: Early identification of emerging technologies through weak signal prediction in higher-order networks
- 作者: Mingzhu Wei, Rong Zheng, Rui Song
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Information Processing & Management
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ipm.2026.105233
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7221779330
- 摘要: The early identification of emerging technologies from weak signals is a critical strategic imperative. Existing network-based methods remain largely confined to lower-order representations and static, snapshot-based conceptualizations of weak signals, emphasizing retrospective detection over prospective prediction. Therefore, this study constructs a multi-source heterogeneous hypergraph with higher-order semantics, spanning the “Science-Technology-Industry” dimensions. By mapping the structural phase transitions of weak signals across an “Outlier-Mutation-Key Node” trajectory, we devise a quantifiable indicator system to shift the analytical paradigm from static retrospective detection to dynamic evolutionary tracking. Using a dataset of 145,316 papers, 79,050 patents, and 5941 project records from the New Energy Vehicle (NEV) fuel cell domain (2015–2024), we identify 226 candidate nodes exhibiting this trajectory. A two-round Delphi expert evaluation confirms 193 of these nodes as expert-confirmed weak signals, establishing Delphi-validated reference labels for subsequent model evaluation. We then employ Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP), Dynamic Hypergraph Convolutional Network (DHCN), and Large Language Models (ZS-LLM and ICL-LLM) to prospectively predict weak signals with disruptive potential. The ICL-LLM achieves the best predictive performance among the tested models, with a Macro-F1 score of 61.25%, outperforming the MLP (49.76%), DHCN (56.84%), and ZS-LLM (46.35%). This synergy between higher-order topology and semantic intelligence effectively filters stochastic noise and enables meaningful predictive capability of emerging technologies, providing an exploratory decision-support framework for technology foresight.

## 2. Navigating the Sea of Reviews: Unveiling the Effects of Introducing AI-Generated Summaries in E-Commerce

- 标题: Navigating the Sea of Reviews: Unveiling the Effects of Introducing AI-Generated Summaries in E-Commerce
- 作者: Qili Wang, Yi SU, Liangfei Qiu, Runyu Chen
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Information Systems Research
- DOI: 10.1287/isre.2024.1282
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7220470690
- 摘要: Amidst the burgeoning incorporation of generative AI into commercial realms, numerous companies are exploring ways to leverage generative AI tools in practice. However, there is still a lack of empirical evidence to examine their effectiveness. In this research, we explore how AI-generated summaries (AIGS), a relatively new generative AI tool, influence consumer review behavior. While one may assume that AI-generated content diminishes users’ incentive to contribute by replacing user-generated content, we find the opposite: the introduction of AIGS actually leads to an increase in the volume of consumer reviews. We propose that this effect is driven by enhanced perceptions of collective efficacy. By highlighting the value of consumer reviews as a collective force, AIGS reinforces contributors’ belief that their individual input meaningfully contributes to a shared body of product knowledge, thus motivating participation. This mechanism is supported by an additional controlled laboratory experiment where we directly measure perceived collective efficacy and find that it mediates the effect of AIGS on the likelihood of leaving a review. Our moderation analysis results reveal that the positive effect of AIGS on review contributions is more pronounced among products that are well-reviewed (vs. under-reviewed) and polarizing (vs. non-polarizing). Moreover, a fine-grained review content analysis shows that reviews after the AIGS introduction exhibit enhanced content richness and provide complementary information beyond the AIGS content. Specifically, contributors discuss fewer product attributes already covered by AIGS, discuss more product attributes not covered by AIGS, and elaborate on AIGS product attributes rather than restating the summary. Taken together, these findings advance our understanding of how AI integration influences consumer review behaviors in e-commerce and offer valuable practical guidance for business strategies on employing generative AI.
