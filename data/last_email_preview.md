- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Aslib Journal of Information Management
- DOI: 10.1108/ajim-11-2025-0871
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7230185431
- 摘要: Purpose This study advances Information Systems (IS) scholarship by theoretically integrating artificial intelligence (AI) and sustainability research, developing a socio-technical value-chain framework that explains how AI generates sustainability outcomes across organizational and institutional contexts. Design/methodology/approach A systematic literature review following a Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses-inspired protocol was conducted. Peer-reviewed articles (2020–2024) were retrieved from major databases, screened through a multi-stage process, yielding 33 eligible studies. Theory-driven coding identified dominant IS perspectives, socio-technical mechanisms, and sustainability trajectories. Findings The review reveals significant theoretical fragmentation characterized by adoption-centric models and limited sustainability integration. Three analytical dimensions emerge: (1) adoption conditions, (2) AI-enabled capability drivers, and (3) multi-dimensional sustainability impacts. AI's sustainability value is mediated by socio-technical configurations, governance structures, and contextual contingencies. Research limitations/implications The study is limited to English-language publications within a defined timeframe. Future IS research should empirically examine the proposed framework using multi-level and longitudinal designs to theorize AI-enabled sustainability as an evolving socio-technical process. Practical implications The framework provides organizations with a structured lens to align AI adoption, capability development, and governance mechanisms with long-term sustainability objectives, thereby mitigating risks of technological misalignment and value erosion. Social implications By emphasizing governance, contextual contingencies, and socio-technical alignment, the study highlights the societal importance of responsible AI integration to ensure that digital transformation contributes to inclusive and sustainable development rather than reinforcing existing inequalities. Originality/value By integrating IS theories, AI research, and sustainability scholarship into a coherent socio-technical model, this study offers a bridging framework that reconceptualizes AI integration as a value-creating process embedded in complex information systems.

## 2. The Ethical Implications of Synthetic Data in Data Publishing: Towards a Coherent Governance Framework

- 标题: The Ethical Implications of Synthetic Data in Data Publishing: Towards a Coherent Governance Framework
- 作者: Junkai Chen
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Learned Publishing
- DOI: 10.1002/leap.2117
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7232545125
- 摘要: ABSTRACT The proliferation of generative AI has made synthetic data, algorithmically generated datasets modelled on, but not directly recorded from, real‐world observations, an increasingly prevalent resource in scientific research. Two defining properties distinguish synthetic data from empirically collected data: virtuality, denoting the absence of direct observational records tied to identifiable real‐world entities and statistical fidelity, denoting the degree to which a synthetic dataset preserves the distributional structure and variable relationships of its source data. While these properties enable synthetic data to address data scarcity and facilitate privacy‐sensitive research, they do not render it ethically neutral: synthetic data inherits—and may amplify—biases embedded in its training data, and in the case of partially synthetic datasets, re‐identification risks are reduced rather than eliminated. Existing ethical rules for data publishing are established around the stages of data submission, review, storage and dissemination, were designed for empirically observed datasets and are ill‐equipped to govern synthetic data, resulting in systematic deficits in transparency, quality control and long‐term reproducibility. This paper argues that addressing these deficits requires a fundamental reorientation of data publishing governance: from a result‐oriented model, in which final datasets are evaluated as static artefacts, towards a process‐oriented approach, in which the scientific rigour and ethical integrity of the data generation methodology itself are subject to systematic scrutiny. Accordingly, this paper proposes a tripartite governance framework: at the submission stage, mandatory synthetic data type declarations and structured generation methodology summaries should be incorporated into data specifications; at the review stage, evaluation criteria should be extended to encompass the transparency, fidelity and reproducibility of the generative process; and at the storage and sharing stage, metadata structures and licensing agreements should be revised to ensure the legally compliant and epistemically responsible circulation of synthetic data within and across research communities.

## 3. Fragmentation as Administrative Burden in Read and Publish Agreements

- 标题: Fragmentation as Administrative Burden in Read and Publish Agreements
- 作者: Christy Jarvis
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Learned Publishing
- DOI: 10.1002/leap.2124
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7230282684
- 摘要: ABSTRACT Read and Publish (R&P) agreements have become an increasingly important mechanism for advancing open access publishing, yet libraries continue to report substantial administrative effort associated with their implementation despite ongoing investments in publisher workflows, institutional tools and community standards. This paper synthesizes literature from scholarly communications, public administration, organizational studies and information systems to examine why administrative burden persists. Building on these complementary perspectives, it proposes fragmentation across publisher ecosystems as a conceptual framework for understanding the operational work required to administer institutional portfolios of R&P agreements. The framework suggests that administrative burden arises not simply from the complexity of individual agreements but from the cumulative effort required to reconcile differences among publisher workflows, metadata, reporting practices, eligibility rules and support models. Viewing libraries through this lens highlights their role in performing interoperability work that transforms fragmented publisher implementations into a coherent institutional service. The paper concludes by considering the implications of this framework for libraries, publishers, consortia and standards organizations, arguing that future progress will depend not only on improving individual systems but also on reducing fragmentation across the broader scholarly communications ecosystem.

## 4. Materiality and investor reaction to environmental corporate social responsibility : A replication and extension of Flammer (2013)

- 标题: Materiality and investor reaction to environmental corporate social responsibility : A replication and extension of Flammer (2013)
- 作者: Steve Kofford, Ben W. Lewis, Myles Melancon
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Strategic Management Journal
- DOI: 10.1002/smj.70135
- URL: https://openalex.org/W4407941808
- 摘要: Abstract Research Summary We replicate and extend Flammer's (2013) event study of investor reaction to environmental corporate social responsibility. Flammer found that investors react positively to eco‐friendly events and negatively to eco‐harmful events, with positive reactions diminishing and negative reactions intensifying from 1980 to 2009. We confirm her results in a narrow replication and find that these time trends persist in a quasi‐replication extending the sample through 2019, despite major shifts in the institutional environment for sustainability investing. We then extend Flammer by incorporating financial materiality and find that the investor reactions and time trends Flammer observed are primarily concentrated in material events. Notably, investors drew this distinction throughout the 40‐year window we study, predating the institutionalization of materiality‐based sustainability analysis. Managerial Summary We examine how investors respond to companies' environmental actions. Analyzing 40 years of data on US‐listed public firms, we find that investors reward companies for eco‐friendly actions and penalize them for harmful ones. Over time, these reactions have shifted: investors have grown less enthusiastic about eco‐friendly actions and more critical of harmful ones. Importantly, investors react primarily to environmental actions that are financially relevant, or “material,” to the business and were doing so well before formal standards existed. Within material actions, positive reactions to eco‐friendly initiatives have weakened while negative reactions to eco‐harmful ones have grown stronger. These findings suggest that managers should prioritize environmental initiatives closely tied to their company's financial performance.

## 5. Great Expectations? Researchers in Germany are Optimistic about the Potential of Grant Lotteries

- 标题: Great Expectations? Researchers in Germany are Optimistic about the Potential of Grant Lotteries
- 作者: Christophe Heger
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Quantitative Science Studies
- DOI: 10.1162/qss.a.517
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7203763513
- 摘要: Abstract In this study I investigate researchers’ expectations towards the potential advantages of grant lotteries in a representative sample of scientists at German universities and higher education institutions provided by the Scientists Survey of the German Centre for Higher Education Research and Science Studies (DZHW). I describe the saliency of the two debates on bias in grant peer review and on grant lotteries, as well as researchers’ levels of experience with grant peer review. I use both descriptive statistics and analytical models to investigate which characteristics shape scientists’ relative grant lottery optimism. I find that experienced and more senior scientists are significantly less optimistic about grant lotteries. Saliency and reviewer workload are positively associated with optimism. I conclude that, even though saliency of the issue remains sparse, researchers are generally optimistic about grant lotteries, yet also aware of some of their potential disadvantages.
