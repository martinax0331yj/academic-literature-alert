## 1. Guardians of the cyberverse: Dispositional, attitudinal, and autobiographical correlates of user engagement against misogynist digital hate

- 标题: Guardians of the cyberverse: Dispositional, attitudinal, and autobiographical correlates of user engagement against misogynist digital hate
- 作者: Rinat Meerson, Kevin Koban, Jörg Matthes
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: New Media & Society
- DOI: 10.1177/14614448261492098
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7223084391
- 摘要: As digital misogyny remains widespread, user intervention is an essential complement to lenient platform governance. Yet, willingness to intervene varies considerably across individuals and cultural contexts. In a cross-national survey conducted in Austria ( n = 791), France ( n = 800), Hungary ( n = 775), and Sweden ( n = 785), we examine how dispositional, attitudinal, and autobiographical characteristics relate to users’ direct and indirect intervention and support for moderation. Empathic anger consistently emerged as the strongest positive predictor of all three outcomes across countries. Perspective-taking, online self-presentation, and victimization experiences showed meaningful positive associations with intervention, while misogynist attitudes were inconsistently related. Toxic disinhibition showed a dual pattern, negatively associated with moderation support but positively associated with direct intervention across all countries, suggesting its potential to be cleverly repurposed toward counterspeech. These findings underscore the importance of emotionally activating literacy programs and highlight the need for culturally sensitive intervention models.

## 2. EXPRESS: Expanding Markets or Capturing Share? The Competitive Effects of Subscriptions on Spending in Restaurant Delivery

- 标题: EXPRESS: Expanding Markets or Capturing Share? The Competitive Effects of Subscriptions on Spending in Restaurant Delivery
- 作者: Daniel Minh McCarthy, Shin Oblander, Young-Hoon Park, Yeohong Yoon
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Journal of Marketing Research
- DOI: 10.1177/00222437261502330
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7222938569
- 摘要: Subscription programs are increasingly central to platform strategy, but their effects on competitors and category demand remain unclear. The authors study restaurant delivery subscriptions using individual-level transaction data on Postmates Unlimited, the first widely available U.S. subscription program in the category, and DoorDash's later DashPass program. Using a matched, staggered difference-in-differences design, they find that signing up for Postmates Unlimited is associated with a 90.4% increase in spending at the focal firm, with no significant average change in competing-firm spending and no offsetting decline in adjacent food categories, consistent with category expansion. Competitive effects depend on customers' prior platform usage. For “singlehomers” (who only used Postmates prior to subscribing), subscribing is associated with increased spending at both the focal firm and competing platforms, even after cancellation. For “multihomers” (who had also previously used competing platforms), the focal-firm effect is larger, and competing-firm spending falls while customers remain subscribed; after cancellation, the focal-firm lift persists, but the decrease in competitor spending does not. DashPass results are broadly similar, but customers with prior Postmates subscription experience show little increase in category spending. Together, these findings suggest subscriptions can both expand markets and capture share, depending on prior platform usage and subscription experience.

## 3. Music Marketing with Generative AI

- 标题: Music Marketing with Generative AI
- 作者: Benedikt Roder, Jochen Hartmann
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Marketing Science
- DOI: 10.1287/mksc.2025.0150
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7221827692
- 摘要: The multibillion-dollar music industry faces severe content creation bottlenecks, making multimedia marketing assets prohibitively costly for smaller artists. This practice paper introduces a Marketing Asset Generation Engine (MAGE) that can produce over 700 million video ad variations reflecting each artist’s visual identity using only a music track’s audio and album cover as inputs. Collaborating with one of the world’s largest music labels, we conduct four preregistered field studies with over 11 million impressions across 75 campaigns for 33 music tracks on TikTok and Meta to validate MAGE. First, pooled across studies, MAGE-generated ads significantly outperform human-designed ads on TikTok with on average 26% higher click-through rates and 10% higher engagement, while we do not detect statistically significant performance differences on Meta, all at 98% lower production cost. Second, neither marketing professionals nor laypeople can reliably identify winning ads such that random sampling of MAGE’s outputs performs as well as human-curated selections. In contrast, an AI-in-the-loop system trained on past performance data can predict pre-launch which MAGE-generated ads will perform best for specific audience segments. These findings demonstrate that generative AI democratizes music marketing by making effective, on-brand video ads accessible at scale.

- 期刊或来源: International Marketing Review
- DOI: 10.1108/imr-03-2025-0169
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7221828629
- 摘要: Purpose This study aims to systematically examine how digitalization reshapes global value chains (GVCs) from a firm-centric perspective. It seeks to integrate theoretical insights and identify the antecedents, transformation processes, outcomes and moderating factors of digital GVC restructuring. Design/methodology/approach A systematic review of 39 empirical articles (2014–2024) integrates the ownership–location–internalization (OLI) paradigm and stakeholder theory to construct a multidimensional framework that captures firms’ strategic responses to digital transformation. Findings The review reveals that digitalization reshapes GVCs through reconfiguration of ownership advantages (e.g. digital capabilities), location strategies (e.g. digital infrastructure and regulatory environments) and internalization mechanisms (e.g. platform integration and blockchain). Outcomes include enhanced firm performance and governance innovation, as well as social and environmental challenges. These effects are moderated by national institutions, industry characteristics and firm-specific capabilities, resulting in uneven digital dividends and risks. Originality/value This study contributes to international business research by (1) linking micro-level firm strategies with macro-level GVC changes, (2) offering an integrated theoretical lens combining OLI and stakeholder perspectives and (3) highlighting underexplored issues such as power asymmetries and ecological trade-offs in digitalized GVCs. It provides a clear agenda for future research and practical recommendations for firms navigating digital transformation in global contexts.

## 5. Antecedents of growth hacking in small and medium-sized enterprises: A technology-organization-environment perspective in developing economies

- 标题: Antecedents of growth hacking in small and medium-sized enterprises: A technology-organization-environment perspective in developing economies
- 作者: Rizaldi Yusfiarto
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Technovation
- DOI: 10.1016/j.technovation.2026.103742
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7222834219
- 摘要: This study examines the growth hacking mechanism and its antecedents in enhancing Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SME) entrepreneurial performance in developing economies. Despite growing interest in growth hacking, prior studies remain limited in providing a comprehensive theoretical explanation and empirical validation, particularly within the context of developing countries. Using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling, this study analyzes data from 318 SMEs that have adopted digitalization practices. Drawing on the Technology–Organization–Environment (TOE) framework, it examines technological factors (big data analytics and digital transformation), organizational factors (entrepreneurial leadership and learning ambidexterity), and environmental factors (government support and university partnership). The findings indicate that big data analytics, digital transformation, entrepreneurial leadership, and learning ambidexterity significantly enhance growth hacking effectiveness. Government support emerges as a key external enabler, while university partnership shows no significant effect. This study contributes to the literature by positioning growth hacking as a cross-functional capability within the TOE framework and highlighting the contextual importance of institutional support in developing economies.
