## 1. To sign or not to sign initiatives to reform research evaluation: A discussion of the pros and cons

- 标题: To sign or not to sign initiatives to reform research evaluation: A discussion of the pros and cons
- 作者: Lutz Bornmann
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Journal of Informetrics
- DOI: 10.1016/j.joi.2026.101872
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7212143433
- 摘要: The integration of bibliometrics into science governance has fundamentally transformed scientific practices, leading to unintended consequences such as goal displacement and citizen bibliometrics. In response, international reform movements like the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment (CoARA) advocate for responsible research assessment and the primacy of qualitative peer review. This short communication examines the commonalities and differences of these movements and weighs the pros and cons for research organizations considering signing them. While endorsement offers socio-political legitimacy and can catalyze internal modernization toward improved evaluation processes, organizations are also confronted with certain challenges. These include the potential neglect of disciplinary evaluation cultures, the high administrative burden of qualitative review, and the prevalent risk of purely symbolic uptake. Acknowledging the limitations of current empirical evidence, this commentary concludes that the future of research assessment relies not on universal declarations, but on the development of process-specific evaluation designs. Strategic principles for implementation – such as tailoring assessments to specific occasions, respecting epistemic diversity, safeguarding data sovereignty, empowering evaluation committees, evaluating assessment procedures, and professionalizing evaluative bibliometrics – are proposed to advance responsible evaluative practices.

## 2. Diffusion-augmented adversarial poisoning in QoS-aware cloud API recommender systems

- 标题: Diffusion-augmented adversarial poisoning in QoS-aware cloud API recommender systems
- 作者: Zhen Chen, Jiajie Ma, Zeyu Tan, Dianlong You
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Information Processing & Management
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ipm.2026.105156
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7212299541
- 摘要: Data poisoning attacks pose a serious security threat to QoS-aware cloud API recommender systems by injecting carefully crafted fake user profiles into training data, thereby manipulating recommendation outcomes and compromising prediction reliability. Existing GAN-based poisoning methods often suffer from discriminator overfitting under limited interaction data, leading to pronounced distributional discrepancies between generated and real users, which significantly degrades both attack effectiveness and stealthiness. To address this limitation, we propose DA-GAN, a Diffusion-Augmented Generative Adversarial Network for poisoning attacks against QoS-aware cloud API recommendation systems. DA-GAN integrates diffusion probabilistic modeling into the adversarial training by constructing multi-step noise-augmented real users through a diffusion forward process, and generating fake users via noise-aligned posterior sampling in the reverse process. To further stabilize training, we design a multi-step noise-aligned discriminator that performs joint discrimination across multiple diffusion levels, providing reliable gradient supervision and effectively mitigating discriminator overfitting in small-sample settings. Extensive experiments on real-world cloud API QoS datasets demonstrate that DA-GAN consistently outperforms state-of-the-art poisoning baselines in both attack effectiveness and stealthiness across multiple mainstream QoS-aware recommender systems. Our findings reveal previously underestimated vulnerabilities in existing cloud API recommender systems and underscore the urgent need for diffusion-aware and attack-resilient defense mechanisms. Our code is available at https://github.com/Ethereal-Fairy/DAGANcode .

## 3. Under-the-radar female migrants in Spain as a user moral economy: collective agency in the platform economy

- 标题: Under-the-radar female migrants in Spain as a user moral economy: collective agency in the platform economy
- 作者: Adriana Calvo Viota, Miren Gutiérrez Almazor, Antonia Moreno
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Journal of Documentation
- DOI: 10.1108/jd-01-2026-0053
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7212361459
- 摘要: Purpose This article examines how undocumented Latin American migrant women in Spain collectively appropriate WhatsApp to navigate intersecting challenges of irregular status, gendered precarity, and exclusion from formal labor protections. Using the lens of the user moral economy, it asks how everyday digital practices produce solidarity, transparency, and fairness while confronting platform and institutional power. The study foregrounds collective agency to show how informal, trust-based networks compensate for the opacity of bureaucracies and asymmetries of algorithmic systems, enabling information sharing, mutual aid, and mobilization that reconfigure platform use toward community survival and dignity. Design/methodology/approach Qualitative, multi-site design combining: (1) 17 semi-structured interviews with Latin American migrant women (regular/irregular status); (2) participant observation in weekly non-governmental organization (NGO) workshops (∼20 participants); and (3) non-participant observation of a closed WhatsApp group (1,437 text interactions, 479 visuals, 1,151 emojis). Data were transcribed, anonymized, and analyzed through thematic analysis with inductive and deductive coding; codes were mapped to four user moral economy elements. Trustworthiness was enhanced via triangulation, a collaboratively developed codebook, intercoder reliability (Cohen’s kappa), member checking, and reflexive journaling. Ethical safeguards (consent, confidentiality) and secure, access-controlled storage were prioritized for a high-risk population. Findings Four interlinked dynamics structure these women’s digital practices: (1) transparency and information sharing (legal procedures, services, jobs); (2) fairness and mutual aid (redistribution, emotional support, housing/work leads); (3) collective agency and tactical appropriation (closed, trust-based groups to mitigate surveillance/exclusion); and (4) repurposing algorithms for community goals (coordination, rapid mobilization). WhatsApp offers an infrastructure that allows them to offset institutional opacity and asymmetries. Yet vulnerabilities persist: precarity, data/privacy risks, and regulatory gaps that exclude undocumented workers. Digital solidarity coexists with ongoing exposure to algorithmic and legal harms. Originality/value The study advances user moral economy theory within platform studies by evidencing everyday algorithmic resistance in private messaging ecosystems. It reframes undocumented women not as passive technology users but as collective actors who co-produce alternative moral orders – transparency, reciprocity and care – within platform constraints. Empirically, it contributes rare, granular evidence on undocumented women’s digital labor ecologies in Spain. In practice, it informs NGOs, policymakers, and platform designers on how to support collective agency and extend protections regardless of legal situation, bridging regulation with lived realities in the gig/platform economy.
