## 1. AI integration and cross-border knowledge sharing in MNEs' international marketing strategies: a systematic review and fact-based narrative perspective

- 标题: AI integration and cross-border knowledge sharing in MNEs' international marketing strategies: a systematic review and fact-based narrative perspective
- 作者: Sonika Jha, Sriparna Basu, Swetketu Patnaik
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: International Marketing Review
- DOI: 10.1108/imr-03-2025-0154
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7204451239
- 摘要: Purpose Artificial intelligence (AI) has become an essential driver of competitive positioning and advantage for multinational enterprises (MNEs). The integration of AI into marketing strategy has transformed how businesses interpret consumer behaviour, optimize campaigns and expand across international markets. We apply institutional theory and strategic choice theory to explore how strategic choice and institutional expectations create a continuous cycle of linked actions that lead to the development and reinforcement of AI integration patterns and practices within an organization, which also go on to underpin the change and transformation organizations have to go through. Design/methodology/approach Using a systematic literature review (SLR) of 156 research papers and a thematic and content analysis of 298 text units from industry-based literature – we synthesize the body of knowledge emerging from both to provide a comprehensive framework for understanding AI's role in the global marketing strategy structure of MNEs. Findings The findings from the SLR and fact-based narratives show convergence in collectively moving away from treating AI as a monolithic input and identifying the specific pathways through which AI-related capabilities shape organizational and market outcomes; reflecting a growing interest in sequential and mediated processes, as opposed to direct effects, suggesting that the value of AI in international and inter-organizational contexts is largely indirect and contingent on intermediate mechanisms such as knowledge digitization, cultural adaptation and dynamic capability development. Practical implications Managers must integrate risk assessments into AI strategy planning and diversify data infrastructure to mitigate over-dependence on politically sensitive regions. They must get into active scenario planning, AI adoption audits and stress-testing business models against plausible geopolitical disruptions. Organizations should weigh geopolitical risk into investment decisions and partnership evaluations from the outset. Originality/value This study integrates academic and industry insights to construct a cohesive framework for understanding AI's role in shaping global market competition. The findings show that MNEs have to develop twin capabilities, both institutional and strategic, to respond to external pressures and regulations. MNEs achieving success with AI integration are not merely viewing this as a technological upgrade, but as a key facilitator for reimagining their entire international marketing strategy structure, which creates agility for the organization to come up with market-responsive strategies in global operations. While mimetic isomorphic pressures encourage firms to adopt proven AI solutions, this does not preclude innovation and the differentiation in this context emerges not from whether MNEs adopt AI, but from with what institutional and strategic logic they deploy it.

## 2. A Temporal Perspective on Corporate Venture Capital Investments' Potential to Drive Innovation

- 标题: A Temporal Perspective on Corporate Venture Capital Investments' Potential to Drive Innovation
- 作者: andreas engelen, Julia Demmer‐Nolden, Verena Rieger, David Lohmar
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Journal of Product Innovation Management
- DOI: 10.1111/jpim.70052
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7204480570
- 摘要: ABSTRACT Corporate venture capital (CVC) investments are a major means by which corporations access information from startups to facilitate their own innovation. While the literature implies a static perspective, we offer a dynamic account that accommodates CVC investments' typical occurrence in staged rounds. Building on and adding to resource dependency theory, this study offers a research model that differentiates temporally between first and follow‐on CVC investments and their effects on the investing firm's product and business model innovations. Adding further temporal nuances, we also investigate whether the degree of relatedness between the investing firm's and the venture's industries affects these relationships. Using data on 708 firms from 2005 to 2019, we show that first CVC investments in new ventures particularly benefit investors' product innovations, while follow‐on CVC investments especially benefit their business model innovations. We also demonstrate that related investments tend to amplify the effects of first CVC investments, while unrelated investments tend to enhance the effects of follow‐on CVC investments. Thus, the study introduces a temporal perspective and highlights the complex interplay among the investment round, the type of innovation, and the relatedness of investments in determining CVC investments' temporal effects on innovation.

## 3. Disintermediation Governance and Complementor Innovation: An Empirical Look at Amazon.com

- 标题: Disintermediation Governance and Complementor Innovation: An Empirical Look at Amazon.com
- 作者: Han Xia, Gaoyang Cai, Grace Gu
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Management Science
- DOI: 10.1287/mnsc.2024.04439
- URL: https://openalex.org/W4375934982
- 摘要: This study investigates how the platform’s disintermediation governance policy (i.e., the policy that disciplines complementors and consumers circumventing the platform to transact directly) affects complementors’ product innovation and launching strategies. We leverage a change in the Communication Guideline on Amazon.com (i.e., the focal platform), which prohibits complementors (i.e., sellers) from sending external website links, telephone numbers, and email addresses to buyers in direct messaging, as a policy shock to Amazon complementors. By conducting a difference-in-differences analysis, we find that the governance policy significantly reduces product innovation on the focal platform among complementors with direct selling channels as measured by the number of new products and the innovativeness of new products. These main effects are mitigated by complementor reputation on Amazon and complementor multihoming. Mechanism analyses show that complementors with direct selling channels increase the number of new products launched through those channels after the policy change (i.e., the switching effect). Moreover, complementors tend to strategically switch innovative and high-value products away from the focal platform while leaving less innovative but price-competitive products on it to attract Amazon consumers before diverting to their direct selling channels (i.e., the bait effect). Our results have implications for platform governance policies regarding disintermediation and its impact on complementor product innovation and launch strategies both on and off the focal platform.
