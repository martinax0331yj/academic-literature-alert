## 1. Digital affordances for customer engagement: The role of co-creation climate, social media manager’s characteristics, and creative brand content

- 标题: Digital affordances for customer engagement: The role of co-creation climate, social media manager’s characteristics, and creative brand content
- 作者: Concepción Varela Neira, Filipe Coelho, Zaira Camoiras-Rodríguez
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Information & Management
- DOI: 10.1016/j.im.2026.104473
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7236632631
- 摘要: Although firms increasingly rely on social media to reach, high platform activity does not automatically yield user engagement and can suppress participation when communication strategies are misaligned. This underscores a central IS challenge: understanding how organizations can mobilize platform affordances to craft content that captures attention and stimulates interaction. Moreover, despite the importance of creativity for innovation and digital transformation, IS research has devoted limited attention to it, especially from a market perspective. As a result, we know relatively little about how organizational practices and managerial capabilities align with platform affordances to foster market-facing perceptions of creativity and, ultimately, customer-contributed value. Addressing these gaps, this study investigates how micro-level managerial capabilities and organizational routines relate to follower-perceived content creativity and multidimensional customer engagement. Utilizing multi-source, dyadic and triadic data from two empirical studies in Spain, we test a multilevel sociotechnical model using structural equation modeling. The findings indicate that organizational co-creation practices, as well as social media managers' task adaptivity and emotional intelligence, are positively associated with content originality and relevance through the enactment of platform affordances. Furthermore, follower-perceived content creativity is positively associated with second-order customer engagement, with market competitive intensity conditioning these pathways. The study advances affordance theory by extending its analytical scope from internal enterprise systems to open digital platforms, while conceptualizing digital creativity as an externally evaluated artifact. Practically, the results suggest that firms cannot rely on raw connectivity alone; managers and organizations must actively orchestrate adaptive iteration and collaborative listening to foster customer-contributed value.

## 2. When GenAI Increases Inequality: Evidence from a University Debating Competition

- 标题: When GenAI Increases Inequality: Evidence from a University Debating Competition
- 作者: Antonio Roldán-Monés
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Management Science
- DOI: 10.1287/mnsc.2025.01063
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7171735205
- 摘要: Research on Generative AI (GenAI) and work reports both productivity-compressing and inequality-expanding effects, leaving open a central question for organizations: Who benefits from GenAI and on which specific tasks? I address this question through a randomized controlled trial in competitive university debating, where participants are randomly assigned access to GenAI. Although most previous experimental work examines the impact of GenAI on written activities and across individuals, this setting allows me to study the impact of GenAI on a knowledge-intensive spoken activity and to identify heterogeneous effects across subtasks. I find that GenAI expands performance inequality in this setting. Consistent with recent theories on GenAI and work, results show that high-ability participants experience significantly larger gains from GenAI usage than their lower-ability counterparts in judgment-intensive subtasks, whereas lower-ability individuals only improve their performance in less expert components of debating, where GenAI can be used as a substitute. If these inequality-expanding effects generalize to similar spoken settings where complex ideas must be internalized and explained, rather than simply written, they would have important implications for understanding the impact of GenAI on work and inequality. This paper was accepted by Maria Guadalupe, business strategy. Supplemental Material: The online appendix and data files are available at https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2025.01063 .

## 4. Blockchain-based services and their perceived usefulness in academic libraries in Southwest Nigeria

- 标题: Blockchain-based services and their perceived usefulness in academic libraries in Southwest Nigeria
- 作者: Babarotimi Opeyemi Oluwaseun, Mpho Ngoepe, Sindiso Bhebhe
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Information Services & Use
- DOI: 10.1177/18758789261510325
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7239732188
- 摘要: Academic libraries require digital services, but evidence on stakeholders’ perceptions of blockchain applications in academic libraries remains limited. This study examined blockchain-based services perceived as suitable for academic library operations and assessed the perceived usefulness of blockchain technology in Southwest Nigeria. A quantitative cross-sectional survey was conducted in 15 purposively selected federal, state and private universities. A structured questionnaire was administered through non-probability voluntary participation, producing 1,166 valid responses from 1,032 faculty members and 134 academic librarians. Data were analysed using frequencies, percentages, means, standard deviations and Welch’s test. Main-study Cronbach’s alpha coefficients were 0.741 for perceived suitability and 0.761 for perceived usefulness. Respondents expressed favourable perceptions of blockchain applications in circulation, authentication, acquisitions, resource sharing, cataloguing, digital rights management, scholarly communication, research data sharing and digital preservation. Perceived usefulness ratings were also favourable among librarians (M = 3.40) and faculty members (M = 3.36), regarding data security, transaction integrity, transparency and digital asset management. No significant differences in perceived service suitability were found across university ownership categories within either respondent group. The study concludes that stakeholders recognise blockchain’s potential relevance to academic library services. It recommends feasibility assessments, professional training, institutional policies and phased pilot projects before wider implementation.
