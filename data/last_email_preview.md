## 1. Corporate brand avoidance scale: Antecedents and consequences in industrial markets

- 标题: Corporate brand avoidance scale: Antecedents and consequences in industrial markets
- 作者: Amélia Brandão, Rui Pinto, Carlos Brito
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Industrial Marketing Management
- DOI: 10.1016/j.indmarman.2026.09.007
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7215060027
- 摘要: This study addresses a significant gap in industrial B2B branding research by conceptualising, developing, and validating a generalisable measurement scale for Corporate Brand Avoidance (CBA). Grounded in Social Exchange Theory and Signalling Theory, the research adopts a sequential mixed-methods design to uncover how and why firms avoid corporate brands across industrial markets. The first study involves a qualitative exploration through semi-structured interviews with managers in the lift industry, chosen as a representative proxy for complex B2B environments, to generate initial insights into the drivers and outcomes of CBA. The second study develops and validates a multidimensional CBA scale using a large-scale survey sampling diverse sectors across the broader industrial value chain, and PLS-SEM analysis. The resulting reflexive six-dimension scale introduces two new B2B-specific dimensions – Deficit-Liquidity Avoidance and Work-Related Avoidance – reflecting financial fragility and operational performance issues that motivate avoidance in industrial contexts. The findings further reveal that corporate brand distrust and corporate brand inauthenticity are the most influential antecedents of CBA, whereas negative corporate brand equity is its most prominent consequence. By demonstrating that negative past experience does not significantly trigger avoidance in long-term B2B relationships, the study highlights the distinctive nature of industrial decision-making. Overall, this research makes a clear contribution to advancing the theoretical understanding of negative corporate brand relationships across B2B contexts and provides a robust measurement instrument applicable across various industrial sectors.

## 2. A platform for change? Core component innovations, complementor adaptation, and multihoming in platform‐based ecosystems

- 标题: A platform for change? Core component innovations, complementor adaptation, and multihoming in platform‐based ecosystems
- 作者: Shiva Agarwal, Ram Ranganathan
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Strategic Management Journal
- DOI: 10.1002/smj.70136
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7222670264
- 摘要: Abstract Research Summary Digital platforms coordinate complementor innovations through interfaces into their core components. However, when interfaces themselves evolve alongside core component innovations, complementors may exhibit behaviors that are not entirely predictable. Using granular data from Apple's five major iOS releases, we abductively examine App developers’ responses following Apple's core component innovations. We document that, in post‐release periods, complementors simultaneously increase co‐innovation on iOS while being more likely to multihome by diversifying to the competing platform. These choices are further explained by whether architectural changes reflect cross‐platform standardization or platform‐specific differentiation. Standardization spurs both complementor co‐innovation and multihoming, while differentiation reduces diversification tendencies. The patterns that we document reveal an important tension: governing through interfaces enables platforms’ scalability but constrains their ability to control complementors as platforms evolve. Managerial Summary Digital platforms like Apple coordinate large scale innovation through standardized interfaces, but those interfaces must evolve as platforms compete, and the direction of that evolution has consequences managers should attend to. Analyzing Apple's five major iOS releases, we find that architectural changes are associated with increased co‐innovation but also a higher likelihood that developers diversify to a competing platform. This pattern is particularly pronounced when updates bring iOS closer to Android, suggesting that architectural convergence may lower the barriers to cross‐platform expansion for developers. Importantly, this behavior is strongest among highest‐value developers. Platform managers should therefore treat architectural evolution not merely as a technical decision, but as a strategic one with direct implications for ecosystem loyalty, competitive differentiation, and the retention of valuable ecosystem partners.

## 3. Competition and Diversity in Generative AI

- 标题: Competition and Diversity in Generative AI
- 作者: Manish Raghavan
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Management Science
- DOI: 10.1287/mnsc.2025.02761
- URL: https://openalex.org/W4405311690
- 摘要: Recent evidence, both in the laboratory and in the wild, suggests that the use of generative artificial intelligence reduces the diversity of content produced. As generative AI penetrates more markets and reaches widespread adoption, should we still expect to see content homogenization? Our work begins with a simple observation: content producers do not act in isolation. Instead, they compete with one another (e.g., for customers or attention). We explore the impact competition has on both content diversity and overall social welfare when producers use generative AI. Through a formal game-theoretic model, we show that competitive markets select for diverse AI models, mitigating monoculture. We further show that a generative AI model that performs well in isolation (i.e., according to a benchmark) may fail to provide value in a competitive market. Our results highlight the importance of evaluating generative AI models across the breadth of their output distributions, particularly when they will be deployed in competitive environments. We validate our theoretical results with a series of experiments involving generative text and image models. Overall, our results suggest that homogenization due to generative AI is unlikely to persist under strong competition. ## 4. Synthesizing event semantics for temporal knowledge graph reasoning

- 标题: Synthesizing event semantics for temporal knowledge graph reasoning
- 作者: T. Li, Yilin Duan, Xun Liang, Qingfeng Guan
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Information Processing & Management
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ipm.2026.105232
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7222513560
- 摘要: Most existing temporal knowledge graph reasoning (TKGR) methods primarily model structural dependencies and temporal dynamics from historical facts, while fact-aligned event semantics, such as hierarchical event types and background contexts, are often underexploited. This may result in incomplete semantic characterization of relations and entities, making it difficult to distinguish semantically similar event relations and contextually plausible entity candidates. To investigate this issue, this paper proposes ES-TKGR, an event-semantics-enhanced model for TKGR, which constructs and integrates event semantics aligned with TKG facts to model implicit dependencies among facts. Specifically, ES-TKGR consists of three key components. First, the E vent T ype H ierarchical C onstraint E ncoding (ETHCE) module incorporates hierarchical event type semantics to strengthen relation modeling by introducing a hierarchical distance-aware prototype contrastive objective over relation representations, while promoting semantic interaction among facts through explicit type-aware connections. Second, the S tatic K nowledge G raph E ncoding (SKGE) module incorporates event background semantics by constructing a global static graph from historical facts and aligning it with background knowledge, thereby capturing time-independent global interaction patterns. Third, an adaptive fusion module integrates dynamic features from ETHCE and static features from SKGE to obtain unified representations. To evaluate future entity and relation prediction under fact-aligned event semantics, we construct four ICEWS-based TKG datasets aligned with event semantic information. Experimental evaluation demonstrates that ES-TKGR outperforms or remains competitive with recent baselines, underscoring the effectiveness of fact-aligned event semantics for enriching entity and relation representations and modeling implicit fact dependencies in TKGR.

## 5. Generative semantic-enhanced vision-language pre-training for sign language translation

- 标题: Generative semantic-enhanced vision-language pre-training for sign language translation
- 作者: Lixin Wang, Yuchun Fang
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Information Processing & Management
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ipm.2026.105234
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7222688963
- 摘要: Vision-Language Pre-training (VLP) provides a promising way to acquire language-guided visual representations for Sign Language Translation (SLT). However, the unique temporal characteristics of sign language videos hinder the effective utilization of text supervisory signals. In this paper, we re-examine the impact of different text data on guiding visual feature extraction in VLP, demonstrating that text more temporally aligned with the videos yields more informative supervision. Based on this finding, we propose a Context-aware Dynamic Semantic Enhancement (CDSE) method to enhance text supervision. CDSE enables text data to simulate the temporal characteristics of the videos by integrating data-driven statistical properties with an adaptive expansion mechanism. Moreover, to reduce text annotation costs, we inject sign language priors into Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate pseudo-gloss sequences from spoken language sentences. We further design a multi-positive contrastive learning method to mitigate the adverse effects of individual low-quality pseudo-glosses. Extensive experiments on two widely used datasets, PHOENIX14T (8257 videos) and CSL-Daily (20,654 videos), demonstrate that the proposed method significantly outperforms the baseline. On the test sets of both datasets, our method improves BLEU-4 by 2.53 and 2.82 points, and ROUGE by 2.48 and 2.08 points, respectively.
