## 1. Proposing a minimal-coding methodology to customize large language models to automate services in information organizations

- 标题: Proposing a minimal-coding methodology to customize large language models to automate services in information organizations
- 作者: Rohit Raj Gunti, Devendra Potnis
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Information Services & Use
- DOI: 10.1177/18758789261496243
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7220856802
- 摘要: Rarely do any studies propose a minimal-coding methodology to customize a Large Language Model (LLM) as part of the AI-driven automation of information services in libraries. This paper fills in the gap by illustrating a four-stage methodology to customize Llama 3 when automating cataloging, chat support, and recommendation services. Firstly, we collected and processed library data, that is, 197 frequently asked questions from the library website of a leading Research-I university in the US, and metadata of 450,320 books and their one-million-plus user ratings from the Book-Crossing dataset. Secondly, we applied the finetuning technique to customize the LLM using 80 percent of the collected data. Thirdly, we tested the customized LLM using the remaining 20 percent of the collected data. We used BERT F1 and ROUGE scores to evaluate the performance of the customized LLM, which confirmed the quality of its output. Finally, we uploaded the customized LLM to HuggingFace, an open-access repository. We present benefits for non-technical information professionals (e.g., minimal-coding methodology), information organizations (e.g., transportability of the proposed methodology, extending and expanding services), and patrons (e.g., privacy). Theoretical implications at the intersection of AI, information organization, and human-information interaction are discussed at the end.

## 2. Uncovering the innovation effects and multiple antecedents of employee role orientations in human-generative AI collaboration: a social information processing perspective

- 标题: Uncovering the innovation effects and multiple antecedents of employee role orientations in human-generative AI collaboration: a social information processing perspective
- 作者: Hongyu Cui, Peng Yu, Hu Tao
- 年份: 2026
- 期刊或来源: Aslib Journal of Information Management
- DOI: 10.1108/ajim-10-2025-0800
- URL: https://openalex.org/W7214062788
- 摘要: Purpose In human-generative AI collaboration (HGAIC), employees can adopt different role orientations to collaborate with GenAI, but it remains unclear how employee role orientations affect their innovation and how they are shaped. Based on social information processing theory (SIPT), this study examines the effects of passive and co-production role orientations on GenAI-enabled employee innovation, the multiple antecedents of these orientations, and the moderating role of employee opportunism. Design/methodology/approach This study used partial least squares structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM) and fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA) to analyze survey data from 409 employees. Findings The results indicate that passive and co-production role orientations positively affect GenAI-enabled employee innovation. GenAI intellectual stimulation and task urgency positively influence passive role orientation, whereas task innovativeness negatively influences it. GenAI feedback richness, GenAI intellectual stimulation, and task innovativeness positively affect co-production role orientation. Opportunism only amplifies the positive effect of passive role orientation on GenAI-enabled employee innovation. Additionally, the fsQCA results reveal three configurations that lead to high levels of GenAI-enabled employee innovation. Originality/value First, this study reveals the relationship between different role orientations in HGAIC and GenAI-enabled employee innovation. Second, this study identifies the antecedents of passive and co-production role orientations from the perspectives of GenAI feedback and task requirement cues. Finally, this study reveals a counterintuitive finding: employee opportunism can act as a positive catalyst for innovation under specific HGAIC conditions.
